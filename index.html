Do you offer cash on delivery?

Yes. We deliver with cash on delivery to more than 300 cities in Pakistan, including Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad.

Can I check the product before I pay?

Yes. Open the parcel and inspect it in front of the rider. Pay only once you’re happy with it.

How long does delivery take?

It depends on your city. Send your city on WhatsApp and we’ll tell you when to expect it.

What do “open box” and “10/10, no box” mean?

Open box items are slightly used and come with their original box and accessories. “10/10, no box” means used with no marks or faults, sold as the item only. Unused repacks are new items in a plain brown box.

Is there a warranty?

Products are covered by our warranty and returns policy. Terms vary by product, so ask us if you want to confirm before ordering.

Can you help me pick between two keyboards?

Yes. Send both names on WhatsApp with what you play and how much you type, and we’ll tell you which one fits better.

Need help picking a switch?

Message us on WhatsApp with your budget and what you play. We reply with two or three options that fit.
